Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Lacrosse Board (shown on the home screen as Lax Board, "the app") is a lacrosse tactics board. It has no sign-up and asks for no name or e-mail address. This policy sets out what stays on your phone and the one case in which anything leaves it.

1. Your plays stay on your phone

Boards, plays, notes and settings are stored only on your device. When you share a video, picture or play file, it goes only where you send it through the iOS share sheet; the app never receives a copy.

2. Share codes

If you choose to create a share code, the plays you have saved — their names, notes and piece positions — are copied to our Firebase (Google Cloud) project in Japan so that people with the code can read them. Nothing else is sent: no name, no e-mail address, no contact list, and you do not create an account.

Your phone is given a random anonymous id. It exists only so that you alone can change or withdraw your own share.

Anyone who knows a code can read the plays behind it, so only give it to people you mean to share with. Codes cannot be listed or searched, so nobody can reach a playbook without being told its code.

"Stop sharing" makes the code stop working and removes the plays from the cloud. Settings → Data → "Delete shared plays from the cloud" also removes the anonymous id. If you never create or enter a code, the app sends nothing at all.

3. Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect device identifiers (including the iOS advertising identifier, only if you allow tracking), IP address, coarse location derived from it, and information about ad interactions and app performance. See Google's policies:

  • https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK) you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice under Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices. You can turn off tracking at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

4. Purchases

"Remove ads" is bought through Apple. Apple processes the payment; the app only learns from StoreKit, on your device, whether the subscription is active. We never see your payment details.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page is updated.

7. Contact

For questions about this policy or the app, and to report anything shared through a share code, write to:

yamasaki@yamaxyama.com
